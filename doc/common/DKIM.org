** SPF

Add to the ~example.com~ zone (and each other hosted domain that sends outbound mail):

#+BEGIN_SRC
@ IN TXT "v=spf1 ip4:198.96.117.252 ~all"
#+END_SRC

Outbound IP: 198.96.117.252

The ~softfail~ (~all) is appropriate until outbound delivery is confirmed
clean.  Tighten to ~-all~ (hardfail) once satisfied.

* Additional Hosted Domains

The DKIM key above covers ~example.com~ only (the value in ~/etc/qmail/me~).
Each additional domain that sends outbound mail needs either:

- The same ~default._domainkey.example.com~ record copied into its own zone
  (fine for low-volume domains sharing the same identity), or
- Its own key generated on the server:

#+BEGIN_SRC sh
DOMAIN=example.com
SELECTOR=default
KEYDIR=/etc/qmail/domainkeys

# Generate key
openssl genrsa -out "${KEYDIR}/${DOMAIN}:${SELECTOR}" 2048
chmod 640 "${KEYDIR}/${DOMAIN}:${SELECTOR}"
chown root:qmail "${KEYDIR}/${DOMAIN}:${SELECTOR}"

# Export public key for DNS
openssl rsa -in "${KEYDIR}/${DOMAIN}:${SELECTOR}" -pubout 2>/dev/null \
  | grep -v -- "^-----" | tr -d '\n' \
  > "${KEYDIR}/${DOMAIN}:${SELECTOR}.pub"

cat "${KEYDIR}/${DOMAIN}:${SELECTOR}.pub"
#+END_SRC

Then add to the domain's zone:

#+BEGIN_SRC
default._domainkey.example.com. 300 IN TXT "v=DKIM1; k=rsa; p=<pubkey>"
@ IN TXT "v=spf1 ip4:198.96.117.252 ~all"
#+END_SRC

* Verification

Once DNS has propagated, confirm with:

#+BEGIN_SRC sh
dig TXT default._domainkey.example.com
dig TXT example.com
#+END_SRC

Send a test message to a Gmail or Fastmail address and check the
~Authentication-Results~ header for ~dkim=pass~ and ~spf=pass~.
